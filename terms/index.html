TERMS OF USE

The ground rules.

Last updated November 28, 2026. These terms cover the Holotable Chaos website and its optional listener accounts.

BetaThe listener accounts system is in beta: features may change, and accounts or data may be reset while it is being built and tested.

ACCOUNTS

Who can sign up

You must be 13 or older to create an account. Keep your Discord or Google account secure; anything done while you're signed in counts as you.

BE DECENT

How to behave

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or sexual content.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, including the hosts or other players.
  • No spam, advertising, scams or attempts to break, overload or get around the site's protections.
  • Only share ally codes and links that are your own.
NAMES

Display names

Display names are unique, so two listeners can't look the same, and you can change yours once every 30 days. Names that impersonate the show or its hosts aren't allowed. We may clear a display name that breaks these rules, and you can then choose a new one straight away. Deleting your account frees your name.

MODERATION

What we can do

Show admins can remove content and suspend or ban accounts that break these rules, with or without an end date. Every admin action is logged. An automatic filter also refuses offensive words, slurs and names that impersonate the show; if it blocks something by mistake, email holotablechaos@gmail.com.

YOUR CONTENT

What you post

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting it on the site you let us show it on the site and mention it on the show. Question of the Week answers become visible to signed-in listeners when the question closes, and the hosts may read them out or feature them. You can edit an answer until its question closes; answers can't be deleted one by one, and if you delete your account they stay without your name. Self-reported details like ally codes aren't verified by us.

THE FINE PRINT

No guarantees

Holotable Chaos is a free fan project, not affiliated with EA, Capital Games, Disney or Lucasfilm. The site is provided as is; features may change or stop, and we can't promise it will always be available. We may update these terms and will change the date above when we do.

CONTACT

Questions

Email holotablechaos@gmail.com. See the Privacy notice for how your data is handled.

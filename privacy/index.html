PRIVACY NOTICE

Your data, plainly.

Last updated November 30, 2026. Questions? Email holotablechaos@gmail.com.

WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

Browsing as a guest

You can use the whole site without signing in. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they receive your IP address and browser details when pages load; see GitHub's and Google's privacy policies.

Playback progress and any draft you type into our forms stay in your own browser. The roster review, feedback and Question of the Week buttons open your own email app; nothing is sent until you send it.

WITH AN ACCOUNT

What we store when you sign in

Accounts are optional and only for listeners aged 13 or older. You sign in with Discord or Google; we never see a password.

  • From Discord: your Discord user ID, username, avatar, email address and whether Discord has verified it.
  • From Google: your name, email address, profile picture and whether Google has verified the email.
  • What you choose to add: a display name, an in-game name, a short bio, your ally code and your SWGOH.GG link, and which picture to show. All optional and self-reported; we don't verify them.
  • Account records: when you joined and last signed in, any admin role, any ban, and an audit log of admin actions. We also keep short-lived counters to stop spam.
  • Question of the Week: your answers (one per question, editable until it closes), your upvotes on other answers, and whether the hosts featured an answer. Streaks and badges are worked out from your answers.
  • Roster review requests: the ally code, SWGOH.GG link and in-game name you enter (self-reported, not verified), your goals and current projects, and the request's status. Only you and the show's admins can see them. No screenshots are collected.
  • Notification switches: on/off choices for future Discord alerts. Every switch starts off.

When you sign in, a session is saved in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

WHERE IT LIVES

Who handles your data

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Profile pictures are never uploaded or copied: when the site shows your picture, your browser loads it straight from Discord's or Google's servers (they can see that request, as with any image they host). Choose “Initials” on your account page to show a coloured circle with your initials instead. Daily encrypted backups are kept in a private GitHub repository for up to 6 months. Only the show's owner can decrypt them.

Discord alerts, when they arrive, will be sent by our Discord bot in the Holotable Chaos server, only if you switch them on. We don't send email from the site, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

WHO CAN SEE IT

Who sees what

Your profile isn't public. Once a Question of the Week closes, the answers become visible to listeners alongside each person's display name and chosen picture; if you haven't set a display name, your Discord or Google name is used, or “Listener”. Answers are hidden from other listeners while a question is open, and only signed-in listeners can read them. The hosts may read out or feature answers on the show. Show admins can see display names, profiles and account dates to moderate the community; only the owner sees email addresses.

Other listeners can't see anything about your account.

YOUR CHOICES

Download, change or delete

On your account page you can edit or clear any profile field, download everything we store as one file, and delete your account.

Deleting removes your sign-in, profile, switches, roles, roster review requests and upvotes straight away. Your Question of the Week answers stay on the site without your name or picture, shown as “Deleted listener”, because other listeners may have upvoted or replied to them. Admin audit entries that mention your account stay so moderation decisions remain traceable, but they no longer link to a profile. Copies in encrypted backups expire within 6 months.

To ask anything else about your data, email holotablechaos@gmail.com.

CHANGES

If this notice changes

We'll update the date at the top and, for meaningful changes, mention it on the site or in our Discord.

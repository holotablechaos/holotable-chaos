Episode description

Zetas are too valuable to waste. In this episode of Holotable Chaos, we're breaking down how to earn more zeta materials, which upgrades are worth the investment, and how to stop spending them on abilities that don't help your roster. We'll cover the best places to farm zeta materials, why you should always have a goal before spending, making sure your upgrades actually benefit the team they're meant for, and the importance of finishing teams instead of leaving projects half-built.

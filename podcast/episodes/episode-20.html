THE SHOW / EPISODE 20

The Mini Era | Who Gets Left Behind?

This week on Holotable Chaos, we’re talking about the Mini-Era in SWGoH and what it means for players trying to keep up with the latest content. With a four-week format, new character requirements, and questions about farmability, is this approach making it easier to keep up—or leaving some players behind? We discuss the GL Mando requirements, the lack of Kyber-level Light Speed Tokens, and the challenges facing players who are trying to build their rosters without spending their way through every new release. Is the Mini-Era a step in the right direction, or does it need some work? Want your roster reviewed, have a Question of the Week answer, or a Chaos of the Week submission?

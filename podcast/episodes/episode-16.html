Episode description

Grand Arena can be one of the most complicated parts of Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes, but having a game plan can make all the difference.

In Episode 16 of Holotable Chaos, Jonny and Gavin break down how they approach GAC, from setting defenses and scouting your opponent to managing GAC resources, choosing a side, and making your attacks. Plus, the most important part of any GAC round: hoping your defense does good.

Episode description

The Era of Myths & Legends has officially arrived in Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes! In this episode of Holotable Chaos, we break down every new character announcement, including Darth Jar Jar, Mara Jade Skywalker, Dark Side Vision Yoda, Starkiller Luke, and more. We also discuss GL Mando & Grogu, share our current farming progress, cover Grand Arena and Conquest, and talk about why this might be one of the most exciting eras SWGoH has ever introduced.

Join us as we break down everything coming in the Era of Myths & Legends and what it means for the future of SWGoH!

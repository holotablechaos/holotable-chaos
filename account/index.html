JOIN THE HOLOTABLE

Sign in with Discord or Google.

No passwords. If your Discord and Google accounts share a verified email address, either one opens the same Holotable Chaos account.

WHAT YOU GET
  • Answer the Question of the Week right on the site, and see everyone's answers once it closes.
  • Request a roster review and follow its status until it airs.
  • Choose your alerts for new episodes and questions, delivered in our Discord.
  • Your profile with your Discord or Google picture, in-game name and ally code.
